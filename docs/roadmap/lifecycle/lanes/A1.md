# LC-A1 Archive and Restore (documents) and readiness correctness

- Wave 1. Branch: `claude/lc-a1-archive`. Depends on: F merged. Allowlist: `../allowlists/A1.txt`.
- Read first: `../PROGRAMME.md` sections 2, 3, 5, 6, 7.

## Objective

A person can archive any document and restore it, with Undo, and readiness counts only what it should.

## Build

1. `packages/services/src/lifecycle/archive/`: `archiveDocument(access, documentId)` and `restoreDocument(access, documentId)` taking a `requireAccess` MANAGE proof on the document; owner only; upsert `document_lifecycle`; audit `DOCUMENT_ARCHIVED` / `DOCUMENT_RESTORED`; product events. Archiving never touches grants, AI choices or case selections (D6).
2. `missions.ts` readiness matching (`loadDocumentInstancesByType` and its callers): a document that is replaced (`supersededByDocumentId` set) or archived is not eligible for automatic matching; an explicitly selected one stays eligible and is reported as flagged (new field on the requirement result, for example `selectedButReplacedOrArchived`); a document not present (Recently Deleted or being removed) is never eligible, even when selected. Empty `read-baseline/A1.json`.
3. Archive and Restore action components under `documents/manage/archive/`, registered under the A1 anchor. Archive runs at once with `UndoStatus`; Restore from the Archived tab.
4. Exports under the A1 anchor in `services/src/index.ts`.

## Red first

- A replaced document still satisfies a requirement today (prove, then fix).
- An archived document satisfies a requirement automatically (prove, then fix).

## Acceptance

Owner can archive and restore; stranger, agency recipient and guessed id refused with not-found; archived document leaves the default list and appears in the Archived tab; explicit selection kept and flagged; readiness results as above; retention clock unchanged; works with tables missing.

## Not yours

Any other read site (A2), Replace (B), delete (C), `listMissionSnapshots` and `listTasks` in `missions.ts` (WP-SB2), G1's field-requirement readiness function.
