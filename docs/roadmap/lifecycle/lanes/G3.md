- Wave 0. Branch: `claude/lc-g3-agency`. Depends on: nothing. Allowlist: `../allowlists/G3.txt`.

## Objective

The person who applied to register an agency can withdraw the application while it is still waiting for review. Platform suspension and termination stay as they are; voluntary closure of an active agency is out of scope.

## Build

1. Verify first how `CLOSED` and `REJECTED` are used in `organizations.ts` and anywhere organization status is read. Then `withdrawOrganizationApplication(db, { actorAccountId, organizationId })` in `packages/services/src/lifecycle/agency/`: only an owner member, only while `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, sets `CLOSED`, deactivates memberships through the existing function, audit `ORGANIZATION_APPLICATION_WITHDRAWN`. If `CLOSED` would be misread anywhere as a terminated agency, stop and record it under Blocked (a new status would need a migration, which only F may add).
2. "Withdraw Application" on the agency page while pending, with a plain confirmation.

## Acceptance
