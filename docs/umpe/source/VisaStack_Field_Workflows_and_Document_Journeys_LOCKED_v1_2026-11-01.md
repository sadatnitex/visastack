# VisaStack -- Field Workflows & Document Journeys
**Locked working baseline v1.1 -- 1 November 2026 (expanded document reality and ordinary journeys)**  
**Scope:** Bangladesh-first visa/travel-document journeys; common agency-assisted, self-managed, B2B, family/sponsor, and employer-managed cases.  
**Status:** Product and field-research baseline, not an assertion of population-wide measured frequencies. The baseline may be amended only with stronger field evidence or changing official procedures. This is not an official visa checklist.

## Evidence legend
- **Verified fact:** Official government/VAC rules or a verifiable, expressly advertised individual agency practice (only for that provider).
- **Documented pattern:** Described in practitioner guidance or multiple provider examples; prevalence not established.
- **Reasoned inference:** Likely journey assembled from the evidence, needing user observation.
- **Unknown:** Population share, costs, duration and error frequencies without a representative field study.

## L1 -- Locked operating model
1. The trigger is a destination/trip/visa need or a question, not a desire to complete a profile.
2. Customer communication (call, WhatsApp, email, office) and durable document custody (phone, folders, physical files, secure portal) are distinct.
3. Source documents are gathered from multiple issuers and holders; receipt is not proof of completeness, validity or suitability.
4. Case preparation is a loop: request → receive → check → explain deficiency → replace/clarify → review/approve → assemble.
5. Government pathways are mixed: online applications/upload, physical originals/copies, appointments, applicant attendance, biometrics, reference numbers, collection or e-visa. Always use the current route-specific authority rules.
6. Traveller, sponsor, customer-facing agency, outsourced processor, employer and official authority are separate actors with differentiated access and responsibility. The government, not the agency, decides the application.
7. A traveller's identity and reusable information outlive a case. A case's submitted evidence set, applicant authorization and outcome must remain an immutable historical snapshot, subject to lawful retention and deletion policies.
8. WhatsApp and paper are not treated as defective users; move sensitive information safely into managed storage without requiring users to change every communication habit.
9. An agent's commercial customer relationship and the traveller's rights/control of personal documents are both material; case access is limited to authorized scopes.
10. Digital workflow cannot claim to issue official visas, hold third-party portal credentials, create government appointments or guarantee approvals without the relevant lawful integration and consent.

## L2 -- Locked broad end-to-end workflow
Need → search/inquiry → initial eligibility discussion → scope, quote and consent → document-specific request → distributed collection → per-document verification → corrections/new versions → form and submission packet → applicant fact-check and approval → official appointment/upload/biometrics/submission → tracking and additional-document requests → decision/passport/eVisa → travel use → retention, sharing, renewal and next case.

Cases branch: self-managed; direct B2C agency-assisted; small agent → specialist/B2B processor; corporate admin/travel desk; family/dependents/sponsor; special-purpose study, medical, business, residency.

## L3 -- Where documents live
**Traveller:** original paper at home/bank/employer; phone gallery and Downloads; WhatsApp/email attachments; local laptop; cloud storage; previous agency copies; official application portal; printed travel wallet. Exact prevalence unknown.

**Agency:** messaging inbox; agent phone and laptop; email; scanned-paper intake; client folder or filing cabinet; shared team drive; case tracker/spreadsheet; specialist partner copies; official application receipts; payment/accounts system; or a controlled case portal. No claim every agency uses every channel.

**Official authorities:** category-specific official forms, portals, document upload and/or physical submission with receipts, biometrics and passport custody as applicable. Do not treat a VisaStack copy as an official filed document unless independently confirmed.

## L4 -- Per-document lifecycle (document != file attachment)
1. **Needed:** requirement identified with country, category, source and check date.
2. **Requested:** named issuer/person responsible and due date.
3. **Created/obtained:** physical original, institution-issued PDF or photo/scan; capture issuer and original custodian.
4. **Received:** channel, received date, sender and case ownership recorded.
5. **Inspected:** readability, pages, correct person, naming, dates, type, authenticity indicators, issuer consistency and category requirements. A readable QR is not automatic proof of authenticity.
6. **Deficient:** wrong period, name mismatch, stale document, missing page, conflicting statement or unsuitable format; send understandable correction request.
7. **Replaced:** keep approved current and superseded versions distinguishable; minimize unnecessary copies.
8. **Approved:** by reviewer and by applicant for factual submissions; mark packet-ready but never imply visa approval.
9. **Submitted:** record exact frozen snapshot of supporting documents, official application/reference and authorized submission channel.
10. **Outcome:** decision, returned originals, eVisa/sticker, conditions and expiry; close with retention/deletion boundary.
11. **Reused or renewed:** stable facts are reused, time-bounded evidence must be newly issued, application-specific statements reassessed.

## L5 -- Representative journeys (hypotheses until case observation)
### A. Independent applicant
Search rules → find old passport photo, documents in gallery/email/home file → obtain fresh bank and employer letters → scan phone or photocopy → upload at official portal or carry originals → repair file-format/size issues → attend as required → save PDF/receipt/reference/decision → next journey reconstruct missing papers.

### B. Frontline agency-assisted applicant
Call/WhatsApp inquiry → agency sends checklist → customer sends partial scans/photos, visits with original if needed → consultant renames/downloads/checks → repeated chasing and correction → case officer assembles official form and packet → applicant checks answers → official submission → updates via chat/phone → return originals and outcome. Higher-maturity agency can replace ad hoc file transfer with private upload link.

### C. Small-agent/B2B processor
Customer provides files to trusted local agent → local agent refers agreed tasks to processing partner → files/clarifications move a second time → partner sends request for missing data → local agent relays to applicant → partner prepares or checks → traveller/agent gives required approvals → official route. Preserve originator/customer ownership and each actor's permitted access; do not invent partner authorization.

### D. Family/sponsor
One coordinator gathers each person's passport, birth/marriage records and travel history → financial sponsor/employer/bank contributes another set → document list combines shared trip evidence with individual-applicant evidence → separate official applications and biometrics where needed → coordinated deadlines and returns.

### E. Employer/corporate
Employee, HR/assistant, manager/approver, external agency, host company and finance each provide approvals/documents → travel request, invitation, employment letter and finance evidence → case officer assembles → applicant verifies personal facts → official submission → company retains travel/expense records under authorized policy.

### F. Repeat traveller
Previous forms, identity details and travel history are useful, but passport, employment, bank evidence, invitation, itinerary and visa rules may change → new case must explicitly compare with previous submissions, retrieve current evidence and freeze a new submission packet.

## L6 -- Documents grouped by behaviour, not just name
- **Stable-ish identity/relationship:** passport data, NID, birth/marriage certificates, qualification (may be superseded/reissued; passport expires).
- **Frequently refreshed:** bank statements, solvency, NOC/employment or business status, payslips, current address, income proof.
- **Trip/case-specific:** invitation, itinerary, host/sponsor letters, hotel reservations, conference confirmations, travel insurance, photos in required format.
- **Government/application generated:** government form PDF, account-generated reference, appointment confirmation, submission receipt, biometrics acknowledgement, requests, decision letter, eVisa.
- **Agency/commercial:** service scope, quote, invoice, receipt, assignment, consent, instructions, correspondence and audit trail; must be separated from official evidentiary files where appropriate.

## L7 -- Frictions and their status
**Strongly supported by official procedures:** physical/digital conversion, form/file requirements, fresh case-specific documents, appointments, submission evidence, authentication and correct applicant details.

**Documented industry problems but unmeasured in Bangladesh:** scattered attachments, chasing missing items, wrong versions, lack of clear next owner, insecure transfers, duplicate requests, disappearing staff-held files.

**Commercially important but not proven for VisaStack:** travellers or agencies will regularly pay for a central document system; direct-agency referrals will accept a platform associated with another travel business; saving minutes translates into collected revenue; clients will maintain a reusable profile without a live need.

## L8 -- Locked product interpretation (not feature authorization)
- People enter through a task, case or request and get an immediate useful result.
- The same human can self-manage one case and use different agencies in others.
- Reusable person-level record and distinct case-level submitted snapshot.
- Secure and accessible exchange; communication may continue in existing channels.
- Every required item has an owner, status, reason for rejection, current version and where original resides.
- No blanket sharing of all personal history with an agency or partner.
- No assertion that an uploaded file is verified, authorized or officially submitted merely because it exists in the system.
- Preserve offline/printed/document-at-centre routes and actual applicant participation.
- Respect retention, revocation, legal holds and case evidence; disposal of duplicates matters.
- Final implementation decisions require gap analysis against current production code and actual observed agency cases.

## L9 -- Required field validation before stronger prevalence claims
Review 15–25 real cases across five modes for qualitative workflow mapping; a much larger representative sample would be required to support population-wide percentages. Create a chronology from inquiry through outcome. For each requested document, record original holder/issuer, source channel, format, upload/copy count, reviewer, corrections, elapsed time, final packet, and disposition. Obtain consent and anonymize. Record within-sample counts and observed frequencies, with mode/country denominators. Do not generalise these to the full market, claim 50–100% occurrence, or fabricate 'most use WhatsApp' or success percentages.

## L10 -- Prevalence discipline: what counts as ordinary reality
These labels distinguish **how central a step is to an applicable process** from **how many people in the population actually do it**. No percentage is asserted. “Routine” does not mean universal, automated, or completed correctly by every agency.

| Practice/step | Locked field classification | Boundary that must not be crossed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A person, family, employer or delegate has a destination/purpose/question | **Foundational:** entry event for the defined journey | Not every inquiry becomes a case, payment or application |
| Relevant requirements must be checked against applicant facts and route | **Foundational:** necessary to prepare responsibly | Individuals/agencies may still skip proper checks; that is not proof of compliant practice |
| At least some information or evidence must be supplied to a visa process | **Routine for document-requiring cases** | Quantity and types of required documents vary radically |
| Records are obtained from existing holders/issuers, not always created in one sitting | **Routine practical pattern**, documented through official evidence requirements | No claim every person has bank letters, employer certificates or scans |
| Phone scans/photos, PDFs, email, upload links, printed copies and originals | **Documented common channels across different routes** | Not all channels are used together or in the same proportions |
| Document checking, corrections, replaced versions, need to resubmit | **Operationally central possible loop** | Not every case has an error; frequency of revisions is unknown |
| Appointment, VAC visit, biometrics, original passport custody | **Route/category-dependent**, often critical | Not universal; some eVisa/identity processes differ |
| WhatsApp, call, email, office inquiry | **Documented agency contact patterns** | Which is the most prevalent channel in Bangladesh is unmeasured |
| Multiple staff, agency referrals and B2B processors | **Operating-model dependent** | Not necessarily present in direct or independent cases |
| Second review, signed applicant verification, access-logged secure vault | **Desirable / documented in individual providers** | Do not misrepresent as standard universal current practice |
| Loss, fraud, serious inconsistency, refusals, appeal, legal hold | **Important exceptions/risks** | Do not assert that most cases encounter them |
| Reuse, renewal, new trip, new employer, new passport | **Recurring lifecycle opportunity** | People do not necessarily return quickly or maintain records proactively |

**Universal design coverage is not market prevalence.** Build an experience that can handle routine and conditional paths without making all applicants complete every branch. The source examples do not establish the requested 50–100% population rates.

## L11 -- Ordinary traveller document journey, in the traveller's own sequence
This is the most defensible **representative sequence** for a document-requiring trip; steps can be skipped, reordered, repeated or handled by somebody else. It synthesises actual official requirements with observed provider-reported service patterns. It is not a single observed customer's transcript.

1. **"Can I travel / do I need a visa / can someone help?"** Traveller searches official or commercial sites, asks a friend or an agency, phones, messages or visits. Often only the destination and intent are settled.
2. **"What exactly do I need?"** Learns the route's requirements and rough service/payment process. A first checklist may need to change after the person's employment, financial or travel circumstances are understood.
3. **"I already have some of these."** Looks for originals in a home folder; passport scan and photo in Gallery/Files/Downloads; old PDFs in email, cloud, laptop or messages; possibly asks family or a former agency for copies.
4. **"I don't have the rest yet."** Requests fresh bank, employer, university, invitation, sponsor or government evidence **only if the route needs it**. Issuers may have their own turnaround, opening hours, charges or authentication process.
5. **"How do I make this digital?"** Uses a phone camera or scanning app, print/scan shop, office scanner, existing institution PDF or photocopy. May transfer phone-to-laptop by email, cable or another app. Preserve entire pages, back sides, legible marks and any required translation where applicable.
6. **"Where should I send it?"** Shares via the agency's established communication route or receives a private upload link; for self-managed journeys selects evidence in an official/authorized application portal. A chat attachment is **not** an official submission.
7. **"Why is it being asked again?"** Somebody finds a missing page, blurry image, expired paper, wrong financial period, different spelling, unsupported format/file size, missing translation or change in application facts. This **may** trigger a repeat of steps 4–6; it is not inevitable.
8. **"Which file is the right one?"** There may now be an original, scan, edited/cropped output, combined PDF, renewed issuer copy and a submission-friendly variant. The current usable version and the original source must be distinguishable.
9. **"What did I actually tell the embassy?"** The traveller verifies material facts and the official form; the final case-specific evidence set is selected and frozen at the appropriate submission milestone. An agency review is different from the applicant's authorization.
10. **"Do I print, upload or go somewhere?"** Follows the country/category-specific route: portal, permitted document upload, printed packet, payment, appointment, personal attendance, biometrics, passport handover. The exact sequence may vary.
11. **"Where is my passport / what's happening?"** Retains official reference, payment/submission receipt, appointment confirmation and relevant tracking mechanism. Status can be checked through appropriate official channels; an agency may relay verified information but must not invent decision updates.
12. **"They asked for something else."** If the official authority requests additional evidence, the original requirement/request, who must provide it, deadline and proof of submission matter. Some cases never receive such a request.
13. **"What was the outcome?"** Receives decision/eVisa or returned passport as applicable; checks spelling, validity and conditions; retrieves/retains documents and handles trip readiness, follow-up or refusal if applicable.
14. **"Now I need it again."** Weeks or years later a repeat application starts; stable facts may be reused, but time-sensitive financial/employment evidence and trip facts often cannot. Past submissions matter for accurate history even when a newer version exists.

**Ordinary branching:** (a) just an inquiry, no document yet; (b) self-service throughout; (c) agency starts or takes over; (d) helper/family/employer coordinates; (e) applicant stops, delays or changes destination; (f) application submitted and decided. Do **not** turn an abandoned inquiry into a fictional active case.

## L12 -- Ordinary agency-side case and document journey
This is a **representative operational pattern**, not a statement that every agency follows a formal SOP or uses a CRM.

| When | Agency-side action that really matters | Artefacts / places information might land | Common decision or handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First inquiry | Ask purpose, dates, passport status and context; decide whether to advise, decline or quote | Phone/chat/email/visitor note, intake sheet | Inquiry → qualified, referred or closed |
| Agreement | Explain service, official vs service fees, applicant obligations, consent and payment | Written/voice instructions, quote, receipt, agency record | Who owns the case and what's included? |
| Checklist | Explain actual route-specific items and responsible person | Chat message, PDF/checklist, portal task | Which papers are needed **for this person**? |
| Collection | Receive originals, scans/photos, PDFs, attachments or secure uploads; ask for more | Staff phone/computer, messages, team folder, paper file, controlled portal | Was a file received? Is the original with the traveller or agency? |
| Internal organization | Identify applicant and case, save/categorize/rename relevant files if needed | Client folder, spreadsheet, email, case dashboard | Can another staff member find the correct material? |
| Review | Compare with current requirements and application facts; identify absent/stale/illegible or inconsistent material | Notes, annotated checklist, review comments | Accept, clarify, reissue, or specialist review |
| Correction loop | Ask applicant, bank, employer, inviter or sponsor for replacement or explanation | Calls/messages, alternative document versions | Who is waiting on whom? By when? |
| Prepare forms/packet | Enter facts into the actual official process and assemble required evidence | Draft forms, PDF, official portal and printout | Is it complete and factually agreed? |
| Submission handoff | Ensure applicant approval and valid process; pay/attend/upload/submit as permitted | Government/VAC reference, fee receipt, appointment evidence | What was truly submitted, by whom and where? |
| Pending | Monitor authorized source; communicate actual updates, not fabricated promises | Portal status, SMS/email, chat updates | Who must next act, and what is the source of status? |
| Outcome & return | Record decision, receive/return passport or digital outcome, reconcile service/payment | Receipt, return handover, decision/eVisa, invoice | Case closed? Originals returned? |
| Aftercare | Supply retained copies if lawfully held, support next trip/renewal, dispose within retention policy | Case archive, authorized traveller record | What can be reused; what must be refreshed/deleted? |

**Roles may collapse.** In a solo operation the same person answers, organizes, checks, coordinates and follows up. In larger agencies lead/consultant, collection staff, processor, reviewer, accountant and case owner may be distinct. B2B subcontracting creates an additional authorized handoff, not a new automatic owner of the customer.

**Document reality, not idealized order:** Someone may email a passport page, later bring the original, then send a clearer phone photograph; the officer may need a combined PDF and a printed copy. The same evidence can exist in several formats and locations. The goal is not to assume agencies will name files neatly or follow an impeccable sequence; the goal is to make the case understandable even when inputs are untidy.

## L13 -- Distinguish original, copies, versions, case use and official submission
A single "document" can refer to different objects. Treat these as distinct concepts:

| Layer | Example | Critical question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source / original | Physical issued certificate, passport, institution-generated PDF | Who issued it? Who possesses it? Is it genuine/unaltered? |
| Capture | Passport photo taken on phone, scanner output, downloaded bank PDF | Is it complete, legible and unmodified as evidence? |
| Working file | Renamed, combined, compressed, converted or correctly redacted copy | Which source/version did this derive from? Was substance preserved? |
| Current accepted case version | The document reviewed against today's requirement | Is it approved for **this case**, not merely uploaded? |
| Historical / superseded version | Previous passport scan, outdated statement or earlier replacement | Why was it replaced and when? |
| Submitted evidence snapshot | Exactly what applicant/authorized submitter supplied to the relevant official route | What was actually submitted, with what reference and confirmation? |
| Officially generated artefact | Appointment, receipt, requested-extra-evidence notice, decision, eVisa | Which authority/system generated it and how is authenticity represented? |
| Custody/return record | Original passport held or returned where route permits | Who physically has it and what proof of handover exists? |

**A source document, an edited working PDF, a case attachment and an official submission are not interchangeable.** Preserve truthful provenance and applicant verification. Never alter facts, stamps, transaction history, dates or official records to improve apparent eligibility.

**Document states need plain language:** Not needed / Needed / Asked for / Waiting on issuer or traveller / Received / Needs better copy / Needs correction or explanation / Under review / Accepted for this case / Replaced / Applicant-approved / Included in submitted packet / Returned or retained / Expired or refresh needed / Archived or deleted under policy. These are logical states, not instructions to add this many UI fields or statuses; a smaller interface may express them.

## L14 -- Real correction and coordination loops worth representing
- **Cannot find:** "I had the PDF before" → search another device/account, request issuer copy or take new scan.
- **Not yet issued:** "My employer/bank hasn't prepared it" → named issuer, due date, new request, wait.
- **Poor capture:** cropped edge, glare, low contrast, missing reverse side or page → retake/rescan.
- **Format/size:** official portal rejects extension/size/layout → create compliant derivative, preserving content.
- **Wrong facts or mismatch:** inconsistent spelling, address, date, employer, relationship or travel plans → explain, substantiate or correct through authorized procedure; do not fabricate evidence.
- **Expired/stale:** new bank period, fresh employer letter, new passport or changed route requirement → request current evidence and keep old submission historically accurate.
- **Multiple contributors:** traveller has passport; sponsor/employer/inviter holds another item → request from actual owner, track waiting and handoffs.
- **Wrong version:** processor receives old PDF after new copy exists → identify latest accepted version, prevent mistaken selection.
- **Post-submission request:** authority specifically seeks missing/additional evidence → record source, deadline, permitted channel and proof of response.
- **Lost context:** customer phones a different team member; all they see is attachments → recover requirement, current status, owner and last agreed action.

The **existence of these loops is operationally credible and some triggers are explicit in official instructions; the frequency of each is unmeasured**. Do not label errors, refusals or fraud as majority experiences.

## L15 -- Practical custody, permission and communication boundaries
- Personal identity and bank records remain sensitive even when someone casually sends them to an agency; safer private uploads should be available without making ordinary calls/messages impossible.
- The traveller may own their personal reusable facts; an agency may have contractual and legal duties to retain certain case records. Neither "traveller can delete everything immediately" nor "agency owns the passport data forever" is a safe default.
- A local sub-agent, specialist, employer, sponsor and processor receive only information necessary and authorized for their role; don't equate customer acquisition with access to unrelated personal history.
- Temporary receipt locations (staff phones, email, message threads, downloads, scan-shop devices, third-party copies) are materially different from the system of record; do not assume deleting a primary document automatically deletes every exported copy.
- Track custody for an original physical passport **when it is actually handed over**; otherwise, do not imply an agency possesses it.
- "Ready for agency", "applicant confirmed", "officially submitted", "awaiting authority" and "decision received" are separate truths. VisaStack must never invent a government confirmation or conflate its own case state with the authority's state.
- Information security and privacy controls are requirements for a safe product, not proof that incumbent agencies already follow them.

## L16 -- The natural document patterns to validate first
The initial field-observation checklist should concentrate on ordinary repeated activities rather than dramatic edge cases:

1. First contact channel and whether any documents are needed before quoting.
2. Where a requested file originally lived: home, phone, email, computer, employer/bank, previous agent.
3. How it was obtained: photocopy, original, issuer PDF, screenshot, scan shop, phone photo/scanner.
4. Where it went next: applicant phone, staff chat/email, private upload, folder, paper binder, official portal.
5. How people identify it: file name, human memory, message date, person name, case number.
6. Whether the first copy was accepted; if not, exact reason and number of replacements.
7. Who requested, produced, reviewed and accepted the evidence; delays between actions.
8. Whether formats were changed (merge, crop, compress, print); by whom and for which destination.
9. Whether a document was reused from an earlier case and whether freshness was rechecked.
10. Which official receipt/reference proves actual submission, and how the final packet can be reconstructed.
11. If passport/originals moved physically, custody and return details.
12. Which documents persist after closure, in whose possession and under what retention obligations.

Record for every observed case **its mode, route and date**. Count only actual observations, and report denominators for each claim; e.g., "9 of 12 observed agency-assisted cases sent one or more files through messages" would be a valid **sample observation**, not "75% of Bangladeshi agencies".

## L17 -- What this baseline does and does not authorize for product design
**It supports:** task-first intake, reusable identity records, document acquisition/capture and safe transfer, context-specific requests, intelligible missing-item corrections, version/provenance handling, ownership of next actions, applicant confirmation, freeze of submitted evidence, permissioned collaboration, original custody when needed, return/retention and repeat-journey reuse.

**It does not establish:** that all travellers need a permanent storage subscription, that customers are willing to maintain detailed profiles before a live need, that all agencies process cases alike, that bank statements or biometric visits apply to every route, that AI must touch every record, that any intermediary can claim an official government status, that an automated form can lawfully submit to all government systems, or that the majority suffer every listed friction.

**Implementation discipline:** first compare with deployed VisaStack code and accepted workflows, then observe real consented cases, then change only demonstrated gaps. These are field-workflow contracts, **not an automatic authorization to add modules or overwrite existing architecture**.
