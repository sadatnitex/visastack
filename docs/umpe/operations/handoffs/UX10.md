# UX10 hand-back: Home on a phone (no migration)

- Owner report (3 Nov 2026, phone screenshots of Home at Everything): everything below the dark Next Step card except Dates That Matter looked weak (2/10), and links everywhere were underlined in a way that read as strange.
- Measured first on the real page with made-up data at 390 px, Everything view: 4.22 screens (Browse 2.36). Three tall count cards (about 190 px each), a large grey ring, lists of underlined links, and plain text for "nothing needs you".
- Cause of the underlines: one site-wide rule underlines a link inside a sentence (a `p` or `li`); the Home lists put their links inside `li` items, so every row link was underlined. A row link is not a sentence link. A generic `plain-link` class is now left out of that rule (`.plain-link`, guarded in `lib/card-links-css.test.ts`).
- What changed (`apps/web/src/app/app/applicant/[id]/page.tsx`, one `WP-UMPE-UX10` block in `globals.css`): on a phone the three counts are one connected strip with the number first (the captions stay for a screen reader and leave the eye; a count that cannot be read says Unavailable in small type so it never breaks mid-word); on wider screens the cards are unchanged; the case card is compact with a small ring beside its words; Needs Your Attention rows and the Upcoming and Recent Activity rows are rows with a chevron and no underline, links in bold text colour; "Nothing needs a decision right now." is a quiet success line with a check mark; footer links ("Dates & Expiry", "Profile History", "Continue Profile", "Manage Sharing") are 44 px rows in the accent colour. No wording, link target, count or rule changed, so the parity map needed no new approved change.
- Result at 390 px, same data: Everything 4.22 to 3.80 screens, Browse 2.36. No horizontal overflow; axe (WCAG A and AA) clean in light and dark on the render.
- Tests: new case in `lib/traveller-composition.test.tsx` (row links carry the plain class, captions are spoken not drawn, the success line), proven red by removing the class from one link; `.plain-link` added to the underline guard. Whole web suite passes (4,989 tests).
- Review (`/code-review high`, 8 findings): fixed the chevron being read aloud, the Unavailable value breaking mid-word, the strip leaking onto desktop, captions hidden from sighted users on desktop, an over-engineered grid, and the test coverage; explained and left: the sentence-link rule's list of exclusions keeps growing by one class (an established pattern here, guarded by a test).
- Not changed on purpose: the three depths, the Next Step card, the Deadline Clock, and the list lengths (the parity map counts them).
- Not verified: a real phone, a screen reader, the live signed-in flow. Not done: a deeper redesign of the profile card and a first-run welcome moment; both are candidates for a later package.
