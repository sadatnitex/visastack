# UX6 hand-back: Add a Family Member inside the group trip forms (no migration)

- Owner request (3 Nov 2026): a group trip should let people team up with anyone, family or not. This package is step 1 of the owner's two-step plan: family members can be added, existing or new, when the trip is created and at any time later. Step 2 (friends and colleagues with or without an account) is a separate package with a database change; its brief is `docs/umpe/operations/handoffs/UX6-companions-brief.md`.
- What changed:
  - Start a Group Trip and Add Travellers each get an "Add a Family Member" section: a name, Family Member or Minor (Guardian), and the same authority confirmation the Home form asks for. It is one quiet secondary button until opened (owner direction, 3 Nov 2026: lean first, secondary choices on request), then opens in place to the name, a compact Relationship choice (Family Member, or Minor (Guardian) as the secondary option) and the single confirmation, with no extra explanatory text. A closed section sends nothing. On a trip where everyone is already added, the page shows the one line and the button, and no empty Add button.
  - The person is made on the signed-in account with the existing service (`createApplicant`, relationship Family Member or Minor only, authority recorded), then added to the trip like anyone else, with their own case and checklist. Nothing is shared, no account is made for them, no default access is given.
  - The dead end "Everyone you manage is already on this trip." is gone: the trip page always offers the family member section.
  - Before anything is made the server checks that every ticked traveller is managed by the account and, on a trip that already exists, that the trip is the account's. A refused try makes nobody.
  - If a step after the person was made fails, the result carries the new person's id (`created` on the form result); the form ticks them so trying again does not make a second record.
  - Focus moves to the name when the section opens and back to its button on Cancel.
- Files: `apps/web/src/app/app/trips/{actions.ts,trip-forms.tsx,trip-overview.tsx}`, `components/form-feedback.tsx` (optional `created`), `app/add-applicant-form.tsx` (exports the authority sentence), `globals.css` (one `WP-UMPE-UX6` block).
- Tests: `trips/family-member-database.test.ts` (real database: made once with authority and on the trip, new person as the only traveller, refused without authority, over-long or empty name, a relationship of Myself refused, ticked traveller of another account makes nobody, another account's trip makes nobody, nothing ticked), `trips/trip-forms.test.tsx` (closed by default and sends nothing, opens with the right fields, focus, Cancel, the empty-list case, the created person ticked). Proven red by removing the authority check and by removing the trip check, then restored.
- Reviews: `/code-review high` found 3 issues, all fixed (a person was made before the trip's ownership was checked; the form remounted and lost its message and tick after a creation followed by a failure; focus was lost when the section opened or closed). The security skill could not start (no origin/HEAD in this checkout), so the security points were checked by hand: the owner of the new record comes from the session, never the form; relationship and name are whitelisted and bounded; every ticked id and the trip are proven before creation; the creation audit event is written by the existing service; the action adds no new route and no email. Pre-existing and unchanged: there is no per-account cap on how many people can be added.
