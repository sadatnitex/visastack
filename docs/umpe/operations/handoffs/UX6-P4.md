# UX6 package 4 hand-back: the companion's own pages, Plan My Own Case, Travel Bookings (migration 0131 written, not run)

Scope: the companion's side of the product and what the organiser sees from it, all behind `UMPE_TRIP_COMPANIONS_ENABLED` (off in production; this package does not turn it on). A helper built it; the coordinator finished the verification and the review fixes.

## What exists
- **Trips You Joined** on `/app/trips` (only with the switch on and at least one joined trip or waiting invitation): a waiting invitation is a plain line with no link (the accept page needs the emailed link).
- **Joined trip page** `/app/trip-invitations/[id]` (the id is the trip; access comes from the signed-in account): read-only, only what `getCompanionTrip` returns (title, destination, purpose, the organiser as "The organiser", other joined people by their confirmed names, "N more travelling with the organiser", never a managed family member's name). Any bad state is the same not-found. On it: **What You Share with the Organiser** (Name Only, Plan Status, Travel Bookings), **Leave This Trip** (asks once), **Plan My Own Case for This Trip**, and, at Travel Bookings, **Choose a Paper**.
- **Plan My Own Case for This Trip**: creates the companion's own case on their own account with the trip's destination and purpose (checklist linked as for trip travellers). Needs core processing consent and the companion's own record (their own record, not a family member's); a person without one is sent through the existing first-run flow with a return. Idempotent (one case per companion row; two tries at once make one). The organiser learns nothing except the one plain Plan Status phrase when the companion chose Plan Status or more.
- **Travel Bookings papers**: the companion picks, from their OWN record, booking, itinerary, invitation and accommodation papers (the common trip evidence kinds; passports, bank and medical papers can never be chosen). The organiser sees the name and kind only, never the file, details or a document id, and only while the level is Travel Bookings and the paper is present and not archived. Lowering the level, leaving, being removed, archiving or deleting the paper withdraws it (going back up never revives a choice). At most 20.
- **Migration 0131** (`umpe_trip_companion_papers`, additive, re-runnable, foreign key names under 63 characters, pinned by a test). **NOT RUN.** It needs the owner's word "migrate 0131". All code tolerates the table being missing: until then Travel Bookings stores the level and shows nothing, as before.

## Pilot gate (investigation only, nothing implemented)
`docs/umpe/operations/handoffs/UX6-P4-pilot-gate.md`: sign-up itself is not gated; the check runs before a new account is made and is bypassed when public beta is open (`PUBLIC_BETA_OPEN`), which the 1 Nov readiness report lists as on. So in production as documented a companion is not stopped. It only matters if sign-up is closed; then matching a link to the gate needs the token carried into the sign-in layer (the security core, not a small change) and matching by address alone would let any organiser admit anyone. Same limitation exists for sharing, client and contributor invitations. Owner decision needed only if sign-up is closed.
