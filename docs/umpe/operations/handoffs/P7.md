## Tables (TEMP migration, three tables, append-only triggers)
- `agency_collection_responses`: org, request, exact revision, link, applicant, submitter, destination, revision number, person name (as sent), consent version, content SHA-256, submitted_at. Never updated.
- `agency_collection_response_items`: item id, kind DOCUMENT, FIELD or MISSING, exact document_version_id or fact_version_id, title as sent. Unique lines (NULLS NOT DISTINCT).
- `agency_collection_response_withdrawals`: one per response (WITHDRAWN or REPLACED). Only a cascade (person, agency, request, document removed) may delete; all three are classified as traveller DATA.
