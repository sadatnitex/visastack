## Tables (TEMP migration)
- `agency_collection_responses`: org, request, exact revision, link, applicant, submitter, destination, revision number, person name, consent version, content SHA-256, submitted_at. Never updated.
- `agency_collection_response_items`: per item, an exact document_version_id, an exact fact_version_id, or MISSING, with the title as sent. Unique with NULLS NOT DISTINCT.
- `agency_collection_response_withdrawals`: WITHDRAWN or REPLACED, one per response.
- All three are append-only. Rows are deleted only by a cascade (person, agency, request or document removed). All are classified as traveller DATA.

## Behaviour
- **Recipient:** comes from P8's desk session through one adapter (`collectionResponseRecipient` on `guestSessionLink`), and `link.ts` uses `sessionProofFrom`. A submit is bound to the link and revision the person reviewed.
- **What the agency gets:**
  - the exact original versions after a fresh sign-in, never a preview (ADR 0022 decision 6)
  - protected details only after a fresh sign-in
  - nothing at all unless the agency is ACTIVE
  - nothing once an answer is withdrawn, replaced or changed, or the request is revoked
- **Audit:** every agency read is audited with counts only.
- **Notice:** "Submitted to Agency" goes in the app and by email to active owners and admins only. It names the agency only, and is checked at send time against the live answer.
- **Limits:** 10 per account and 30 per request per hour, counted only once an answer is valid.
