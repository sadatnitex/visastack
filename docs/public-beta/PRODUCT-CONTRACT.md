# Public beta implementation contract

Authority: [owner directive](../product/public-beta-transformation-directive-2026-10-10.md) and [execution protocol](../governance/autonomous-engineering-qa-protocol-2026-10-10.md), recorded 2026-10-10. This contract indexes those decisions; it does not replace their requirements.

VisaStack is a traveller-owned reusable mobility record. Identity maps to an account, people/family, immutable facts and evidence versions, missions, purpose-limited grants, and reviewed outcomes. Mission remains the canonical case entity.

Decisions: Neon Auth for identity; Resend for auth SMTP and app transactional email; Neon PostgreSQL with Drizzle for canonical data; retain Next.js, services, policy, adapters, audit and durable jobs. Founding Access is complimentary for travellers and agencies, with measured fair use and future billing compatibility. Admission, object authorization, operational safety and entitlement must be evaluated separately.

Preserve default-deny permissions, explicit consent, revocable narrow sharing, exact-version snapshots, transactional audit, human approval of AI suggestions, protected-field encryption, legal holds and recoverable work. Never infer agency wallet access from membership or email equality.

Historical WorkOS defaults, invite-only product assumptions and payment/commercial-certification coupling are superseded as target architecture. Historical evidence remains intact; current code is not silently described as migrated. Public activation, sensitive-document intake, production migrations, key rotation and paid upgrades still require the owner's approval for the particular operation.

Roles: traveller, authorized family/contributor, agency owner/member/case manager, platform administrator, unauthorized visitor. Each role's complete journeys are tracked in [acceptance evidence](TEST-AND-ACCEPTANCE-EVIDENCE.md).

Not in scope: a second record database, case model, policy engine, notification system, billing catalog, broad visual redesign or Bangla translation.

Delivery order: [traceability](IMPLEMENTATION-TRACEABILITY.md). Current scope and continuation: [checkpoint](EXECUTION-CHECKPOINT.md). No claim of unrestricted beta readiness follows from a successful cloud setup.
