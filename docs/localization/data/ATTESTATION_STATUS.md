# Attestation status: what was observed, what was recommended, what was never verified

Generated by `tools/attestation-report.py` from `VISASTACK_PLATFORM_LANGUAGE_EVIDENCE.csv` and `data/BN_BD_CORE_GLOSSARY.csv`. Nothing here was written from memory: every attested row was re-fetched and its Bengali text found in the source (`tools/evidence-verify.py`).

## 1. The three kinds of wording in this work

| Kind | What it means | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|
| OBSERVED | The exact Bengali was read in a product string file or page source and found again on re-fetch. Only `OFFICIAL_STRING_RESOURCE`, `DIRECT_SCREEN` and `BILINGUAL_OFFICIAL_PAGE` rows count | 265 evidence rows; 79 of them have English that matches the intent exactly |
| LANGUAGE-MODEL RECOMMENDATION | Proposed by a language model, not observed anywhere | 90 of 127 glossary terms and every one of the register drafts (full sentences included) |
| NEVER VERIFIED | The source was named in the brief but could not be read | Facebook, bKash, Pathao, myGov, e-Passport, Google Drive, Microsoft Terminology (section 4) |

## 2. What the observed rows actually are

- 248 rows come from Mozilla Firefox string files (community Bengali localizations in `mozilla-l10n/firefox-l10n`): 92 in locale `bn-BD` and 156 in generic `bn`. They are real product strings, but they are a browser, not a Bangladeshi service product, and they are short interface words.
- 17 rows come from the signed-out Google Accounts pages served with `hl=bn` (generic Bengali, not region-specific). They reproduced when re-fetched with redirects followed.
- Most observed rows are NOT the same meaning as the English intent they were gathered for. The `intent_match` column says so: `EXACT` (same English), `RELATED_CONTAINS_INTENT_WORDS` (same word inside a longer string) or `LOOSE`. Only `EXACT` rows are used to call a glossary term observed.
- No observed row is a full sentence, a consent text, a legal sentence or a visa-requirement sentence. No Bangladeshi government, bank, ride or messaging product is observed.

## 5. What this means for the register

Every Bengali sentence in `VISASTACK_TRANSLATION_REGISTER.csv` is a language-model draft (`provenance` column). The register never calls a draft attested. Where a draft uses a glossary term, the term may be OBSERVED_EXACT_FORM (short words such as Save, Delete, Sign in, Password, Settings, Next, Cancel, Close, Try again, Learn more), but the surrounding sentence is not. Words with the highest product risk (the state words, file safety, requirement levels, sharing and legal hold) have no observation at all and rest on the guardrails and the owner as editor.
